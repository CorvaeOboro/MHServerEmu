Calamity is a collection of custom encounters that are short, small, and play like the game's existing "terminals." Each encounter is a one-shot scenario populated by customized enemies wielding augmented powers shaped into cross, line, and cascading patterns, with phase-based chimera abilities.

Encounter: Vampire Blood Ritual

  • Region: Hulk Busters Hightown Arcade (entered via Cloak NPC in Avengers Tower hub)
  • Win condition: Defeat the Vampire Blood Lord
  • 30 trash mobs round-robin from 4 vampire thrall types with random start offset
  • 3 minibosses randomly chosen from a pool of 8 (no duplicates)
  • 1 boss at the ritual site with a floating Amulet of Quiox centerpiece
  • All enemies are spawned once at start; no respawns
  • Minimap markers hidden for all event enemies

Boss: Vampire Blood Lord

Rendered as Scarlet Witch wearing the DarkWanda costume. Uses Scarlet Witch powers for normal single-target casting so animations match the SW pawn. Non-SW augmented powers are cast directly by the boss with an animation reset fired immediately after to mask T-posing.

  • 3-phase enrage: Normal (100-66%), Enraged (66-33%), Furious (33-0%)
  • Cooldowns accelerate per phase: 100% → 70% → 40% of base cooldown
  • Heals 15% max HP on each phase transition
  • Phase 0 (100%):Summons 2-3 vampire thrall
  • Phase 1 (66%): One-time Onslaught Mental Orb cast
  • Phase 2 (33%): One-time Blade JustStayDown cast
  • Round-robin augmented power cycling so the boss rotates through her kit
  • Untauntable, CC resistant, sees through invisibility

Augmented Power Patterns

The boss wields an AugmentedPowerController that casts powers in geometric patterns around the battlefield. Each power has its own cooldown and damage scale. Patterns include:

PatternDescription
Cross4 points on the ground in a cross around the boss
RadialBurstN points evenly distributed in a circle around the boss
CascadingRadialMultiple expanding rings at increasing radius with delays between rings
CascadingLineN points in a line along the boss's facing direction, with delays between each cast
SingleAtPositionSingle cast at a specific position (target or boss position)

Miniboss Pool

3 minibosses are randomly selected (no duplicates) from the following pool each run:

Malekith , Death Gambit , Wolverine , Moon Knight , Grim Reaper , Black Widow , Daredevil , Deathstrike

Vampire Thrall Types

Trash mobs are distributed round-robin from these thrall types with a random starting offset so each run has a different distribution:

  • Dark Elf - melee thrall with red nameplate
  • Purifier - ranged thrall
  • Butcher - heavy melee thrall
  • Dark Elf Assassin - stealth assassin thrall

Ritual Centerpiece

A floating Amulet of Quiox is spawned at the ritual site with looping VFX . The amulet is the thematic centerpiece of the encounter, eluding to its lore as an artifact that can slow vampirism. The coven is attempting to corrupt it.

Play

  1. Go to Avengers Tower hub
  2. Find Cloak NPC (Red name Herald of Darkness)
  3. Interact with Cloak to be transported
  4. Defeat trash mobs, minibosses, and the Vampire Blood Lord to complete the encounter
Note: The event is a one-shot encounter. All enemies spawn once at start. The region shuts down when vacant after completion.
