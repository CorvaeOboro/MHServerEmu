Item Merge Best Affix

merge pair of item into one with the best rolled values from both sources

Merges pair of item into one , taking the best rolled values from each source . Consumes both source items plus costing one Cosmic Essence . this is useful for artifacts , medals , and uniques , merging collected duplicates towards perfect over time .

Commands

CommandDescription
!merge anyMerge one pair from inventory .
!merge <itemType>Restrict to an item type . e.g. !merge ring , !merge medal
!merge <rarity>Restrict to a rarity . e.g. !merge unique , !merge cosmic
!merge dryrunPreview eligible pairs without consuming anything .
!merge dryrun <filter>Dry run with a filter . e.g. !merge dryrun unique
